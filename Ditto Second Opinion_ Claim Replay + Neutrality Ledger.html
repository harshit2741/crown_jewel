Anonymised. Your logged sessions are highlighted.

How this works: the verdict engine ranks plans by risk-adjusted cost (five years of premium, plus expected out-of-pocket for your household, plus half of the worst single bill). Commission is stored with each session only so that it can be audited. The Protected Outcome Rate replays one likely claim in policy year 4 for each session and counts it as protected when the insurer pays at least 80% of the bill. The 160 background sessions are generated by the same engine from random household profiles. All plans, insurers, prices and market shares are fictional.
